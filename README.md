# 🦥 Solace Sanctuary

> **The Low-Friction, Adaptive Student Sanctuary & Life Tracker**  
> *Built for Hacktoberfest 2026: Week 3 – Slow & Steady (International Sloth Day Theme)*

---

## 🖤 The Problem: Student Overload & Decision Paralysis

As college students balancing coursework, exams, skill-building, personal habits, and daily life, there are days when we feel completely overwhelmed. Traditional productivity tools often make this worse—they treat every day like a 100% energy day and force you to manually track every single detail, turning task management into yet another chore. 

When you're running on low energy, opening a complex planner creates instant burnout and guilt.

---

## 🌿 The Solution: What is Solace?

**Solace** is an anti-overwhelm, low-friction student workspace designed to work *with* your energy levels, not against them. Instead of pressuring you on low-battery days, Solace adapts to how you feel, automates manual logging, and keeps you moving forward without the friction.

### ✨ Key Features

- **🔋 Energy State Selector ("Vibe Check"):** Toggle between `High Energy`, `Normal`, and `Zombie Mode`. When you're drained, Solace automatically scales down your daily goals (e.g., downscaling a 1-hour workout to 10 minutes of light stretching or 3 hard coding problems to 1 quick concept review).
- **🧘 Central "One-Next-Step" Focus:** Eliminates decision paralysis by highlighting **ONLY ONE micro-task** at a time. Too tired? Click *"Break It Down"* to split it into a tiny 2-minute step.
- **📝 Smart "Mind Dump" & Auto-Tracker:** Write or paste messy, unfiltered thoughts, daily notes, or voice transcripts. The AI parses the text and automatically extracts:
  - Today's Journal/Diary Archive
  - Auto-checked Daily Habits (Hydration, DSA, Workout)
  - Tomorrow's Action Items
- **🎨 Dynamic Theme Switcher:** Personalize your sanctuary with built-in aesthetic modes:
  - 🖤 **Midnight Sanctuary:** Deep dark glassmorphism & subtle developer glows.
  - 🌸 **Pastel Blossom:** Soft warm cream, rose pink, and lavender accents.
  - 🌿 **Sage Matcha:** Earthy, soothing, and minimalist greens.
- **🎮 Game-Style Boot Sequence:** A cinematic, distraction-free intro screen to help you ground yourself before entering your workspace.

## 🚀 Getting Started Locally

Follow these steps to run **Solace** locally on your machine:

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone [https://github.com/sakshi-db/solace-sanctuary.git](https://github.com/sakshi-db/solace-sanctuary.git)
   cd solace-sanctuary

2. **Install dependencies:**
   ```bash
   npm install

3. **Start the development server:**
   ```bash
   npm run dev

4. **Open in browser:**
   Navigate to http://localhost:5173 to experience Solace!
